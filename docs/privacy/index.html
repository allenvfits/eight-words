Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026

Our privacy approach

Eightwise does not require an online account, show advertising, use third-party analytics, or send your learning activity to us.

Information stored on your device

Your learner profiles, selected difficulty, daily progress, saved and learned words, quiz and test results, points, streaks, and unlocked rewards are stored locally on your device using Apple’s on-device preferences system. This information is used only to provide the app’s features and is not transmitted to us.

Vocabulary catalog

The current release includes its vocabulary catalog inside the app and does not contact a vocabulary service. If optional online catalog updates are introduced in a future release, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that feature is enabled.

Subscriptions

If you purchase Eightwise Plus, Apple processes the purchase. The app checks your subscription entitlement through StoreKit so it can unlock paid features. We do not receive your payment-card information. Apple may process purchase information under its own privacy policy.

Data sharing and tracking

We do not sell personal information, share personal information with advertisers, or track you across apps and websites.

Retention and deletion

Local learning data remains on your device until you remove the app or clear the app’s data. Deleting the app removes local data but does not cancel an active Apple subscription; subscriptions can be managed in Apple Account settings.

Children

Eightwise is designed for a broad audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Eightwise support page.
