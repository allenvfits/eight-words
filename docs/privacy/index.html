EIGHT WORDS

Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026

Our privacy approach

Eight Words does not require an account, show advertising, use third-party analytics, or send your learning activity to us.

Information stored on your device

Your selected difficulty, daily progress, and saved words are stored locally on your device using Apple’s on-device preferences system. This information is used only to provide the app’s features and is not transmitted to us.

Vocabulary updates

The app may contact our Supabase-hosted vocabulary service to download published words. These requests do not include your saved words, learning progress, name, email address, or an account identifier. Supabase may process standard network information, such as an IP address and request metadata, to deliver and secure the service.

Subscriptions

If you purchase Eight Words Plus, Apple processes the purchase. The app checks your subscription entitlement through StoreKit so it can unlock paid features. We do not receive your payment-card information. Apple may process purchase information under its own privacy policy.

Data sharing and tracking

We use Supabase only as a service provider for vocabulary delivery. We do not sell personal information, share personal information with advertisers, or track you across apps and websites.

Retention and deletion

Local learning data remains on your device until you remove the app or clear the app’s data. Deleting the app removes that local data. Removing the app does not cancel an active Apple subscription; subscriptions can be managed in Apple Account settings.

Children

Eight Words is designed for a broad audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Eight Words support page.
