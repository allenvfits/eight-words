Open English WordNet 2025

The extended vocabulary catalog contains a filtered and reformatted subset of Open English WordNet 2025, created by the Open English WordNet community and derived from Princeton WordNet.

Open English WordNet is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License. Eight Words adds difficulty groupings, stable identifiers, formatting, safety filters, and original curated entries. No endorsement by the contributors or their institutions is implied.

Suggested citation

John P. McCrae, Alexandre Rademaker, Francis Bond, Ewa Rudnicka and Christiane Fellbaum (2019). English WordNet 2019 – An Open-Source WordNet for English. Proceedings of the 10th Global WordNet Conference.
